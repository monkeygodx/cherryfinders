How it works

We do the hunting.
You do the choosing.

Apartment locating sounds too good to be free — so here's exactly how it runs, start to finish, and how everyone gets paid.

Start Your Search
/ 01

Tell us what you want

Fill out the quick form: your metro, target neighborhoods, budget, bedroom count, move-in date, must-haves, and dealbreakers. About sixty seconds. The more specific you are, the tighter your shortlist comes back.

/ 02

We build your shortlist

Your licensed agent searches live listings and off-market specials, calls properties to confirm real availability and pricing, and emails you a curated shortlist with honest notes — the good, the catch, and the fine print. Usually within 24–48 hours.

/ 03

Tour & apply

Like what you see? We schedule tours around your calendar — in person or virtual — and come along on the details. When you find the one, we walk you through the application and paperwork so nothing gets lost.

/ 04

Sign & collect your bonus

Sign your lease through us and collect a move-in bonus — up to $200 cash back or a moving credit, depending on the property. That's our thank-you for letting us help.

The honest part

Why it's actually free.

Apartment communities budget a referral fee for filling their units — the same fee they'd pay any locator or listing site. When you sign a lease we found for you, the property pays us that fee. That's the entire model.

You are never charged, and your rent is not marked up to cover us — the fee comes out of the property's marketing budget, not your pocket. Our incentive is simple: help you land somewhere you're genuinely happy, because we only get paid when you sign.

What's included

Everything, for zero.

Daily monitoring of new listings, price drops, and off-market specials across your metro.

The vetting

Honest notes on every option — hidden fees flagged, "too good" rents explained, before you tour.

The legwork

Tour scheduling, application help, and a real person on call from first message to move-in day.
