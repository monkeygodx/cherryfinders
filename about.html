About

Apartment hunting,
minus the hunting.

Cherry Finders is a Texas apartment locating service built on one idea: finding a place to live shouldn't cost you time, money, or your sanity.

Most renters do it the hard way — a dozen browser tabs, unanswered calls, tours that turn out nothing like the photos, and a "special" that quietly adds $200 in fees at signing. It's a second job, and it's stacked in the property's favor.

We flip that. Tell us what you want and a licensed local agent takes over the search — pulling from live listings and off-market deals, calling to confirm what's real, and handing you a shortlist with honest notes instead of a wall of listings. You tour the good ones, we handle the paperwork, and you move in.

Free, and honest about why

Apartment communities pay a referral fee to fill their units. When you sign a lease we found, that fee comes to us — out of the property's marketing budget, never out of your pocket, and never as a markup on your rent. You are the client; the property pays the bill. Because we only get paid when you're happy enough to sign, our interests and yours point the same direction.

Real agents, real license

Every search is handled by a licensed Texas real estate agent — a real person who knows which buildings are worth touring, which "deals" hide a catch, and how to advocate for you through the application. Cherry Finders runs the platform and the front door; licensed agents do the locating and represent you in the transaction.

Where we work

We search the major Texas metros — Dallas–Fort Worth, Houston, Austin, and San Antonio — plus surrounding cities across the state. If you're renting in Texas, chances are we can help.

Honest by default

We flag the catch before you tour, not after you sign. If a place isn't right, we'll tell you.

Fast on purpose

Good units go quick. Most shortlists land within 24–48 hours so you don't lose the one you wanted.

On your side

We're paid to fill units well, not fast. That means fewer regrets and a place you actually like.
